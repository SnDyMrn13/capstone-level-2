Endangered Birds of the World

Birds are necessary for the environment and for ourselves

Birds are inspiring and motivating. They are also important for the environment. They help with "pest" control. They help with seed dispersal which helps with tree growth and forests - as well as plant growth, like in meadows and the "grasslands".
